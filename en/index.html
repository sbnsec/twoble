How it works

One phone, two players

No second device, no Wi-Fi, no account. Just someone sitting across from you.

  1. Lay the phone flat

    Between the two of you, on the table, like a tiny game board.

  2. One side each

    Each player gets half of the screen. Text and scores turn to face them, so nobody reads upside down.

  3. Play, then rematch

    Quick rules are explained before every game. One match, one rematch, and again.

The games

8 games to face off

Skill, strategy and a pinch of luck. Two players face to face, or solo against the AI.

Air Hockey

Game of skill · 1 or 2 players

  • Hit the puck, guard your goal: first to 7 goals wins.
  • Real rules: face-off, 7-second foul.
  • A single round, or best of 3, 5 or 7.
  • 7 table designs, 5 sound themes.
Air Hockey rules and tables

Volte-Face

Strategy game · 1 or 2 players

  • Trap your opponent's pieces: they flip over and switch sides.
  • 8 × 8 board, most pieces wins.
  • Possible moves are shown, so you learn as you play.
  • 4 sound themes: jazz lounge, zen garden…
Volte-Face rules and strategy

Tic-Tac-Toe

Strategy · 1 or 2 players

Drawn by hand: ballpoint pen, marker, chalk, neon… Classic, vanishing (your marks fade away!) or on a big board.

Mancala

Sowing game · 1 or 2 players

Sow your glass marbles, fill your store. Classic, quick, big board or oware.

Four in a Row

Strategy · 1 or 2 players

Drop your discs into the upright grid and line up 4. Discs fall and bounce; at the end of the round, the trapdoor opens.

Dots and Boxes

Strategy · 1 or 2 players

Connect the dots, close a box… and play again! Small, classic or big board.

Marble Pop New

Real-time duel · 1 or 2 players

One half of the screen each: gather six marbles with the same symbol, and pop! Pebbles rain down on your rival. Plus a Journey through 12 arenas.

Yacht New

Dice game · 1 or 2 players

Five real 3D dice: shake, throw, keep the good ones. Three rolls per turn, a 12-box scorecard, and the highest total wins.

Foldable screen

Made for iPhone Duo

Unfolded and laid flat, iPhone Duo becomes a real little table: the fold lands right on the center line, and each player gets a whole panel.

  • Big inner screen: more room to play, the table and board fill the whole display.
  • The fold in the middle: it splits the two sides, like the center line of a real table.
  • Outer screen: folded, the app fits the small screen too.
  • Auto pause: fold, unfold or turn the phone and the game waits for everyone to get back in place.

No iPhone Duo? On any other iPhone, on iPad and on Android, just set the device down between you.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which games are in Twoble?

8 two-player games: Air Hockey, Volte-Face (the piece-flipping game), Tic-Tac-Toe, Mancala, Four in a Row, Dots and Boxes, Marble Pop (a real-time duel of glass marbles) and Yacht (the dice game, with five 3D dice).

Is Twoble free?

Yes. Twoble is free and has no ads. No account needed.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All 8 games work completely offline: on the train, on a plane, on vacation or at the bottom of the garden.

Can I play alone?

Yes. Every game can also be played against the AI, with three levels: easy, medium and hard.

Can we find out who's best?

Yes. Each player gets a profile, a name and an avatar kept on the phone, with no account. Twoble counts wins and head-to-head records, keeps an Elo leaderboard and a podium, and there are 55 badges to earn as you play. Badges can also become Game Center (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Games (Android) achievements if you like.

Which devices does Twoble run on?

iPhone and iPad (iOS 15 or later) and Android (Android 7 or later). The app is optimized for iPhone Duo and foldable phones.

Is it suitable for kids?

Yes. The app is rated 4+, has no ads and collects no data. The rules are explained simply before every game, a two-voice comic tells them too, and a help option can show the good moves.

Which languages?

14 languages: English, French (France and Canada), Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. The app follows your phone's language.

Family game night, friends, date night, road trips: grab someone, set the phone down, and game on.
