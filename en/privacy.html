Privacy policy

Twoble · updated October 30, 2026

In short: the app collects no data.

The Twoble app

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking tools.
  • The app works fully offline: nothing is sent over the internet.
  • Your settings (theme, volume, game options) stay on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Children

The app is rated 4+. Since it collects nothing, it collects nothing about children either.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no ads. Fonts and images are served by the site itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data (such as IP addresses) to keep the service secure: see GitHub's privacy statement.
